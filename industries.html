Industries We Serve

AI Intelligence Systems Deployed Across Public Safety, Infrastructure and Enterprise Operations

Government & Law Enforcement

Ubivis designs and deploys AI-powered intelligence systems for public safety and law enforcement agencies, supporting continuous monitoring, incident detection, and coordinated response across urban and regional environments.

Key Applications:

  • Crime prevention and investigation support
  • Public safety monitoring and threat detection
  • Integrated command centers and emergency response
  • Intelligence analysis platforms

Benefits:

Improved situational awareness, faster response times, better coordination between agencies, and data-driven decision-making in mission-critical scenarios.

Transportation and railway environments require continuous monitoring, safety enforcement, and operational intelligence across stations, tracks, depots, and transit corridors.

Key Applications:

  • Railway surveillance and safety
  • Vehicle tracking

  • Predictive maintenance and asset management
  • Passenger safety and security systems

Benefits:

Enhanced passenger safety, reduced service disruptions, improved asset utilization, and proactive maintenance planning through real-time analytics.
