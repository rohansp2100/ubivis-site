About Ubivis AI Technology

Designing and Deploying AI Intelligence Systems for Government, Infrastructure and Enterprises

Who We Are & What We Do

Ubivis AI Technology LLP is an Indian technology company specializing in the design, integration, and deployment of AI-powered intelligence systems for public safety, infrastructure monitoring, and enterprise operations.

Our focus is on real-world systems - surveillance platforms, command & control centers, and analytics solutions - that operate under constraints of scale, security, reliability, and regulatory compliance.

We work closely with government agencies, infrastructure operators, and enterprises to build solutions that integrate seamlessly with existing systems and deliver actionable intelligence in mission-critical environments.

Vision

To build dependable AI intelligence systems that enhance public safety, operational efficiency, and decision-making for governments and enterprises, while adapting to evolving technological and regulatory requirements.
